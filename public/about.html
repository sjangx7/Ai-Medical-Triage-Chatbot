About This Project

The AI Assisted Triage Bot is a rule-based digital health triage system developed to demonstrate safe, explainable clinical risk classification.

Clinical Approach

The system uses broad symptom classification, red-flag emergency detection, severity scoring, duration analysis, and associated symptom mapping to determine risk levels.

Why Rule-Based?

Unlike black-box AI models, this system provides transparent reasoning behind every recommendation, supporting explainable healthcare technology.

Important Disclaimer

This system is intended for educational and research purposes only. It does not replace professional medical diagnosis. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 999 immediately.
